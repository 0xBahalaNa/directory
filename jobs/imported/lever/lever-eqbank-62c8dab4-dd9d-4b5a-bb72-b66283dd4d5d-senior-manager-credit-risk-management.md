The Work

The Senior Manager, Credit Risk Management reports to the Senior Director, Credit Risk Management and supports Senior Management with respect to all aspects of credit risk management in the Bank. The Senior Manager will be responsible for assisting in the development and operationalization of credit risk strategies and will oversee all loan portfolios with the objective of maintaining high quality lending and risk mitigation strategies.

Key aspects of the role are governance, strategic initiatives, and managing key internal relationships enterprise-wide to champion risk and credit culture at the Bank.

### The Core Responsibilities

Credit Risk Governance

- Provide independent and effective challenge to credit policy and credit procedure changes, amendments, and work collaboratively on new developments as proposed by the Business Lines

- Monitor the loan portfolios for relevant trends, insights and provide ad-hoc credit risk reports to members of senior management and to support quarterly board reporting

- Liaise and maintain strong relationships with the credit functions across the business lines to ensure that credit policies are adhered to in the organization

- Monitor exception frameworks in the business lines and escalate relevant trends and insights

- Support in managing responses to recommendations/requests from OSFI and Internal Audit to ensure they are timely and accurate

- Support the Senior Director, Credit Risk Management and Quality Assurance leaders in ongoing and continuous improvement initiatives to generate operational efficiencies

- Lead Quality Assurance initiative and ongoing reviews for Merchant acquiring business unit

- Provide effective challenge of changes to policy/procedures/processes proposed by the Retail business units and make recommendations to Senior Director, Credit Risk Management

New Products and Initiatives

- Ensure proposed products are in alignment with the bank’s credit risk appetite as articulated in credit policies and procedures and that satisfactory risk mitigation strategies have been discussed and documented

- Review and recommend Credit Risk support of new initiatives to the Senior Director, Credit Risk Management
