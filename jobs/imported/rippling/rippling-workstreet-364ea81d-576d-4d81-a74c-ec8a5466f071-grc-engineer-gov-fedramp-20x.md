# About Workstreet At Workstreet , we’re on an exciting journey to help businesses scale securely by designing and implementing cutting-edge security and compliance programs. As a fast-growing startup, we specialize in a wide range of GRC (governance, risk, and compliance) services that support frameworks across SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, CMMC, NIST 800-171, NIST 800-53, and FedRAMP. We empower companies to meet regulatory requirements and enhance their cybersecurity posture from day one.

Get to know the GRC Engineering (GOV) Team Our GRC engineering team guides defense contractors and federal organizations through their CMMC, NIST SP 800-171, NIST SP 800-53, FedRAMP, and Assessment & Authorization compliance efforts. We act as our clients' trusted guides and primary point of contact end-to-end, leading them through gap assessments, System Security Plans, POA&Ms, and C3PAO/3PAO coordination with clarity, composure, and a genuinely client-first mindset. Beyond the technical depth in RMF, CUI/DFARS requirements, and GovCloud environments, what defines us is how we work: we translate complex requirements into plain language, manage escalations with urgency and care, and take real pride in making every client feel informed, supported, and well-prepared. We're a group that mentors one another, holds a high bar for quality, and thrives in a fast-paced environment where our work directly strengthens the security of the defense industrial base. ## The Opportunity Workstreet is seeking a GRC Engineer (FedRAMP 20x) who is highly motivated, detail-oriented, and possesses foundational knowledge of FedRAMP 20x, CR26 rules, and Key Security Indicators (KSIs). Operating as a key technical contributor within our government delivery practice, this role focuses on hands-on technical execution, building automated evidence pipelines, authoring machine-readable artifacts, and supporting organizations through the FedRAMP 20x certification lifecycle. The successful candidate will combine strong technical execution skills with the organizational discipline to manage multiple compliance tasks concurrently under senior guidance. In this role, you will partner directly with client engineering and compliance leads, assist with 3PAO assessment preparation, and help implement continuous monitoring pipelines to ensure clients achieve and sustain compliance during U.S. Eastern Time business hours. ## What You'll Do - Execute FedRAMP 20x control validation - analyze and apply FedRAMP Consolidated Rules (CR26) and the 46 Key Security Indicators (KSIs) across 10 families to ensure software architectures align with federal standards. - Author machine-readable certification artifacts - create, update, and maintain Security Decision Records, Security Configuration Guides, and OSCAL/JSON/YAML documentation conforming to RFC-0024 and OMB M-24-15 mandates. - Implement automated evidence pipelines - design and maintain automated evidence-gathering workflows to continuously validate KSIs, moving away from manual point-in-time audit procedures. - Conduct readiness and gap assessments - perform technical readiness reviews and control mapping across FedRAMP 20x Class A, Class B, and Class C baseline requirements. - Support Collaborative Continuous Monitoring (CCM) - assist in connecting CSPM and GRC tooling into agency pipelines to feed real-time KSI validation data. - Facilitate external 3PAO assessments - coordinate evidence submission and operational logistics during Third-Party Assessment Organization (3PAO) audits and independent assessments under senior guidance. - Assist in security control remediation - collaborate with internal and client technical teams to remediate security gaps and control deficiencies across multi-cloud environments. - Track evolving federal regulatory updates - monitor CR26 updates, Significant Change Notifications (SCNs), and the Rev5-to-20x transition timeline to keep client programs aligned. ## Who You Are - Federal compliance practitioner - bring 2+ years of direct technical experience executing GRC deliverables across NIST SP 800-53, FedRAMP, or federal authorization lifecycles. - FedRAMP 20x framework familiarity - foundational understanding of FedRAMP 20x Program Certifications (Class A/B/C), CR26 rules, and KSI-based validation models. - Machine-readable artifact author - hands-on experience creating or working with technical compliance artifacts utilizing OSCAL, JSON, or YAML schemas. - Sovereign cloud environment navigator - practical exposure to major cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP) and secure government environments like AWS GovCloud or Azure Government. - Compliance-as-code contributor - working ability with scripting languages or policy-as-code tools (Python, Rego/OPA, Terraform) to help build and inspect automated evidence pipelines. - Disciplined project operator - highly organized practitioner with the ability to coordinate multiple federal compliance tasks concurrently without losing detail. - Effective client communicator - possesses strong written and verbal English communication skills suited for direct collaboration with client technical teams and assessors. ## What will help you succeed - Active security credentials - hold recognized certifications such as CompTIA Security+, CGRC, CAP, or Associate of CISSP. - Cloud infrastructure certifications - technical certifications such as AWS Solutions Architect Associate, Azure Security Engineer Associate, or GCP Associate Cloud Engineer. - Direct 3PAO assessment exposure - practical history participating in 3PAO audits or supporting live agency Authority to Operate (ATO) evaluations. - Collaborative continuous monitoring experience - background supporting FedRAMP ConMon or real-time Collaborative Continuous Monitoring (CCM) workflows. - Complementary defense framework knowledge - baseline familiarity with CMMC 2.0 or NIST SP 800-171 requirements for defense supply chain contractors. ## What We Offer - Career Development : Clear path with mentorship and training opportunities. -
