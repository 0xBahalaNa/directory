Head of Security Compliance & Authorization Second Front Systems (2F) is seeking a Head of Security Compliance & Authorization to lead the security programs that protect our Game Warden platform and enable its use across national security environments. Reporting to the Vice President of Security, this leader will own corporate compliance programs (SOC 2, ISO, CMMC, etc.) and security authorization for the Game Warden platform (RMF/FedRAMP).. You will lead the Security Authorization team, establish priorities and operating rhythms, and ensure the organization has the processes, technical capabilities, and evidence required to support secure product delivery and maintain government authorizations. You will work closely with Product, Engineering, Platform, Legal, and business leadership to integrate security throughout the product lifecycle. This includes secure architecture, application security, vulnerability management, control implementation, authorization strategy, assessment readiness, and continuous monitoring. This is a leadership role for someone who can build durable programs, develop high-performing teams, and bring structure to complex security and compliance requirements. The successful candidate will understand both cloud-native engineering and government authorization, with the judgment to balance mission delivery, customer commitments, technical risk, and regulatory expectations. Note: Candidates must reside in our DC/Maryland/Virginia hiring hub This is a full-time position.

What You'll Do

• Lead the Security Authorization and Compliance team. • Establish the product security and compliance strategy for Game Warden in alignment with the company’s business objectives, product roadmap, customer commitments, and enterprise security strategy. • Define ownership and operating processes for security authorization and continuous monitoring, including the handoffs with Engineering and Security Operations. • Build a security roadmap that identifies capability gaps, required investments, staffing needs, technical dependencies, and measurable outcomes. • Provide leadership oversight for security authorization packages supporting Department of Defense, federal, and other national security environments. • Ensure authorization packages, control implementations, assessment evidence, POA&Ms, and continuous monitoring activities remain accurate, current, and defensible. • Establish the standards and quality controls used by Security Authorization Specialists to manage eMASS packages, DISA PPSM registrations, assessment evidence, findings, and authorization documentation. • Direct readiness activities for security assessments, third-party reviews, government audits, and authorization decisions. Ensure teams address issues before they become assessment blockers. • Own the corporate audit calendar. • Partner with Engineering and Product leadership to integrate security requirements into product planning, architecture decisions, release processes, and engineering priorities. • Track and report vulnerability/configuration findings against authorization and compliance impact of significant changes. • Review significant product and infrastructure changes to determine their effect on security controls, authorization boundaries, inherited services, customer commitments, and existing approvals. • Create a coordinated compliance approach across applicable frameworks, including DoD RMF, FedRAMP, CMMC, SOC 2, ISO standards, and other customer or market requirements. • Reduce duplicate work by establishing reusable controls, shared evidence, common documentation, and cross-framework mappings. • Expand the use of automation for control validation, evidence collection, continuous monitoring, compliance reporting, and authorization-package maintenance. • Establish meaningful metrics for product risk, vulnerabilities, remediation performance, assessment readiness, POA&M health, authorization milestones, and program capacity. • Provide the Vice President of Security and executive leadership with clear reporting on material risks, program status, resource constraints, and decisions requiring leadership attention. • Manage relevant vendors, assessment partners, penetration testing providers, and GRC technology investments. • Coordinate with Security Operations and Incident Response teams when events affect authorizations or attestations.

What You Bring

• Have 10+ years of progressive cybersecurity experience, including significant responsibility for product security, cloud security, security engineering, compliance, or government authorization programs. • Five or more years of experience leading cybersecurity professionals, with demonstrated success setting priorities, developing talent, managing performance, and scaling teams. • Experience owning security programs for a cloud platform, software-as-a-service product, platform-as-a-service environment, or similarly complex technology organization. • Demonstrated success integrating security into product development and engineering workflows without creating unnecessary barriers to delivery. • Strong knowledge of application security, cloud security architecture, vulnerability management, secure software development, and software supply chain risk. • Practical understanding of AWS, Kubernetes, containers, CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure as code, identity and access management, logging, observability, and cloud-native security patterns. • Experience establishing or governing application security capabilities such as SAST, DAST, software composition analysis, secrets detection, container scanning, and penetration testing. • Direct experience with government security authorization programs, including NIST SP 800-53, NIST SP 800-37, DoD RMF, FedRAMP, or comparable federal requirements. • Experience overseeing the development, assessment, authorization, and continuous monitoring of complex authorization packages. • Familiarity with eMASS, DISA PPSM, DISA STIGs, SCAP Compliance Checker, ACAS, IAVM requirements, and the DISA Cloud Computing Security Requirements Guide. • Experience leading formal assessments and working with Security Control Assessors, third-party assessment organizations, government sponsors, and Authorizing Officials. • The ability to evaluate technical risk, understand competing priorities, and make sound decisions when requirements, timelines, or ownership are unclear. • Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to explain technical and regulatory issues to engineers, customers, assessors, and executives. • Experience defining program metrics, presenting risk to senior leadership, and using data to guide staffing, investment, and remediation decisions. • Demonstrated ability to build repeatable processes and operating structures in a fast-moving environment. • CISSP or an equivalent advanced cybersecurity certification required. • Must meet applicable DoD 8140
